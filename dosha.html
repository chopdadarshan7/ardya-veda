Vata, Pitta & Kapha: The Sacred Triad

Everything in the cosmos — from stars to human cells — is woven from five primal elements: Ether, Air, Fire, Water, and Earth. In the human physiology, these express themselves as three governing forces called Doshas.

1. Vata Dosha (वात)

वायु + आकाश
Elements: Air + Ether (Space)

"Movement, rhythm, and cellular inspiration. The force of kinetic energy."

Biological Governance:

Nervous system impulse transmission, cardiac rhythm, peristalsis in intestines, breathing, creativity, and sensory perception.

Signs of Aggravation (Vata Imbalance):

Chronic constipation, dry rough skin, cracking joints, restless insomnia, anxiety, racing overthinking, cold sensitivity.

Pacifying Pathya (Food & Herbs):

Warm, soupy, oily dishes with pure A2 cow ghee. Sweet, sour, and salty tastes. Herbs: Ashwagandha, Bala, Shankhpushpi, warm sesame oil.

2. Pitta Dosha (पित्त)

अग्नि + जल
Elements: Fire + Water

"Transformation, intellect, and digestive combustion. The fire of Agni."

Biological Governance:

Metabolic rate, enzymatic digestion, body temperature regulation, visual clarity, liver detoxification, and critical intellect.

Signs of Aggravation (Pitta Imbalance):

Hyperacidity, heartburn, inflammatory red skin rashes, premature graying, irritability, anger, overheating, loose stools.

Pacifying Pathya (Food & Herbs):

Cooling, sweet, bitter, and astringent foods. Basmati rice, coconut oil, cucumber, fennel, coriander. Herbs: Shatavari, Amalaki, Brahmi, Neem.

3. Kapha Dosha (कफ)

पृथ्वी + जल
Elements: Earth + Water

"Structure, cohesion, and immune protection. The grounding reservoir of Ojas."

Biological Governance:

Joint lubrication (Shleshaka), structural musculature and bones, deep cellular immunity (Ojas), emotional endurance, and affection.

Signs of Aggravation (Kapha Imbalance):

Excessive weight gain, sluggish metabolism, chronic sinus mucus, morning fatigue, depression, hoarding, attachment.

Pacifying Pathya (Food & Herbs):

Light, warm, dry, pungent, and bitter foods. Mung bean soup, roasted veggies, ginger, black pepper, honey. Herbs: Trikatu, Guggulu, Tulsi.
