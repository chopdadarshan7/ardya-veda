त्रिदोष · Your Constitution

Know Your Nature. Heal Accordingly.

According to Ayurveda, you were born with a unique biological blueprint—your Prakriti. It is the precise ratio of Vata, Pitta, and Kapha that governs your cellular metabolism, digestion, and emotional temperament.

Vata

वात
Air + Ether

"Movement & adaptability. The force of kinetic energy."

Qualities: Light, dry, mobile, subtle, cold
Governs: Nervous system, breathing, circulation
When Imbalanced: Anxiety, insomnia, bloating, dry skin
Explore Vata Protocol

Pitta

पित्त
Fire + Water

"Transformation & intellect. The fire of digestion & vision."

Qualities: Hot, sharp, light, oily, penetrating
Governs: Digestion (Agni), metabolism, sharp focus
When Imbalanced: Acidity, skin inflammation, irritability
Explore Pitta Protocol

Kapha

कफ
Earth + Water

"Structure & stability. The grounding cohesive force."

Qualities: Heavy, slow, steady, soft, cool, oily
Governs: Immunity (Ojas), joint lubrication, calm mind
When Imbalanced: Lethargy, congestion, slow metabolism
Explore Kapha Protocol

षड्रस · Clinical Precision & Food

Shad Rasa: The Harmony of Six Tastes

Ayurvedic dietetics is not about counting calories. It is about balancing the six tastes (Shad Rasa) to naturally regulate endocrine balance, digestive enzymes, and cellular hunger.

मधुर

Madhura (Sweet)

Earth + Water

Nourishes tissues, builds Ojas, grounds Vata & Pitta.

Ghee, basmati rice, dates, wheat
अम्ल

Amla (Sour)

Earth + Fire

Ignites digestive Agni, enhances saliva, pacifies Vata.

Amalaki, lime, pomegranate, yogurt
लवण

Lavana (Salty)

Water + Fire

Maintains electrolytes, softens tissues, stimulates hunger.

Saindhava (rock salt), black salt
कटु

Katu (Pungent)

Fire + Air

Clears mucus, stimulates sluggish Kapha, burns Ama toxins.

Ginger, black pepper, pippali, mustard
तिक्त

Tikta (Bitter)

Air + Ether

Cooling detoxifier, cleanses liver, cleanses blood, clears heat.

Neem, turmeric, bitter gourd, fenugreek
कषाय

Kashaya (Astringent)

Earth + Air

Tightens tissues, stops excess secretions, pacifies Pitta/Kapha.

Triphala, green tea, pomegranate peel
Daily Guidance: Include all 6 tastes in your main midday meal to prevent cravings and keep your metabolic fire (Agni) completely steady.
